# Electrocompetent cells

### Day 0 and Day 1 are the same as section 1.1

### Day 2 (all day)

1. Prepare 1 L of growth media, by adding 1 L of LB to a 2-L baffled sterile flasks and adding appropriate drugs.
2. Inoculate 5 mL starter culture. Starting OD should be 0.02-0.04. Incubate 37 ºC / 220 rpm
3. When the OD reaches 0.4-0.6, remove it from the incubator. (Il would measure OD first at the 2-h mark, and then every 20 min after OD rises above 0.2),
4. Incubate culture on ice for 20 min. From here forward, it is important to keep the cells cold, either by working on ice or in a cold room. Have 4x 250mL tubes pre-chilled on ice, and pour culture into the 4x250 mL tubes.
5. First spin. Spin cells down at 3000 g / 15 min / 4 ºC.
6. Decant supernatant off and add 25 mL of cold MPW (should be autoclaved) to each 250 mL tube and resuspend cells. Combine two into 50 mL falcon tubes to get 2x50 mL.
7. Second spin. Spin cells down at 3000 g/ 15 min / 4 ºC.
8. Decant supernatant off add 50 mL of cold MPW to each tube and resuspend cells.
9. Third spin. Spin cells down at 3000 g/15 min / 4 ºC.
10. Decant supernatant off and add 50 mL of cold 15% glycerol to each tube and resuspend cells.
11. Final spin. Spin cells down at 3000 g/15 min / 4°C (Whilst waiting, label up 40 microfuge tubes or 8-track PCR tubes and put them in a cold room).
12. Decant supernatant off and add 4 mL 15% glycerol to each falcon tube, and resuspend cells (Note that if this is for transforming a library and if you intend to use all your competent cells at once, you can also do final resuspension with as little as 1 mL, and transform nearly all of your cells in 2 electrocuvettes (take 450 μL per electroporation).
13. Aliquot 105 μL of competent cells per tube using the repeater & 1mL combi-tip (use slowest settings, and touch combi-tip to tube wall to help dispensing).
14. Flash freeze by dumping tubes into a dewar with liquid nitrogen.
15. Store -80°C.
