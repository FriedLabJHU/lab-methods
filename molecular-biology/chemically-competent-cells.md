# Chemically competent cells

### Day 0 (evening)

1. Get colonies of the cell you'd like to make competent cells of.

   > If simply propagating a commercial line of cells, skip this step.  Take an aliquot of commercial cells (like NEB-10beta or BL21(DE3)) and directly innoculate it into LB media.  **Important Note: In the Fried Lab, to minimize the number of mutations that can accumulate upon serial passages, we always recommend that if you want to propagate commercial competent cells, go back to the original commercial source.  Do not propagate from a lab-grown aliquot.**

   > If trying to convert a strain you have created to become competent, then start by selecting a colony, and innoculating it into a 5-mL overnight culture supplemented with the relevant drugs (go on to step 2).

2. If you want to make plasmid-harbouring cells competent, first start with competent cells, transform (see transformation protocol) with a sequence-verified plasmid, spread 1-10 µL onto LB Agar (+ appropriate drugs), and incubate overnight at 37 °C.

### Day 1

1. Morning: take the agar plate out of the incubator and put in at 4 °C;
2. Evening: pick one colony with a sterile tip, and inoculate it into a 14 mL culturing tube with 5 mL LB (+ appropriate drugs). For 3 L scale, inoculate into 50 mL falcon tube with 15 mL LB;
3. Incubate overnight 37 °C / 220 rpm.

### Day 2: (all day)

Preparing buffers. While waiting during step 4. Store them in an ice bucket.

> Note: All the buffers and MPW should be autoclaved before use.

Keep the burner on while running all Day 2 steps.

1. 200 (600) mL of 100 mm MgCl<sub>2</sub>. Make by diluting 1M MgCl2 ten times with MPW;
2. 200 (600) mL of 100 mM CaCl<sub>2</sub>. Make by diluting 1 M CaCl2 ten times with MPW;
3. 60 (180) mL of 85 mM CaCl<sub>2</sub>, 15% glycerol. Make by adding

| Component | For 1 L Scale | For 3 L Scale |
|---|---|---|
| 1 M CaCl2 | 5 mL | 15 mL |
| 20% glycerol | 45 mL | 135 mL |
| MPW | 10 mL | 30 mL |

1. Prepare 1 (3) L of growth media, by adding 1 L of LB to 2-L baffled sterile flasks (x3) and adding appropriate drugs.
2. Inoculate 5 mL starter culture to each liter of day culture. Starting OD should be 0.02-0.04. Incubate 37 °C / 220 rpm.
3. When the OD reaches 0.4-0.6, remove it from the incubator. (measure OD first at the 2-h mark, and then every 20 min after OD rises above 0.2).
4. Incubate culture on ice for 20 min. From here forward, it is important to keep the cells cold, either by working on ice or in a cold room.

   4a. For 1 L, have 2x500 mL tubes pre-chilled on ice, and pour culture into the 2x500 mL tubes.

   4b. For 3 L, have 6x500mL bottles pre-chilled on ice, and pour culture into the 6x500 mL tubes.

5. **First spin:** Spin cells down at 3000 g/15 min/4°C (4000 rpm on JLA-10,500) .
6. Decant supernatant off and

   6a. For 1 L, Add 100 mL of cold 100 mm MgCl<sub>2</sub> to each 500 mL tube and resuspend cells; to get 2x100 mL in 2x500 mL tubes.

   6b. For 3 L, Add 100 mL of cold 100 mM MgCl<sub>2</sub> to each 500 mL bottle and resuspend cells. Combine three to get 2x300 mL in 2x500 mL bottles.

7. **Second spin:** Spin cells down at 2000 g/15 min/4 °C (3300 rpm on JLA-10,500).
8. Decant supernatant off and

   8a. For 1 L, Add 100 mL of cold 100 mM CaCl<sub>2</sub> to each 500 mL tube and resuspend cells.

   8b. For 3 L, Add 300 mL of cold 100 mM CaCl<sub>2</sub> to each 500 mL bottle and resuspend cells.

9. Incubate on ice for 20-40 min.
10. **Third spin:** Spin cells down at 2000 g/15 min/4 °C (3300 rpm on JLA-10,500).
11. Decant supernatant off and

    11a. For 1 L, Add 25 mL of cold 85 mM CaCl<sub>2</sub>, 15% glycerol to each 500 mL tube, resuspend cells, and transfer into 1x50mL falcon tube.

    11b. For 3 L, Add 75 ml of cold 85 mm CaCl<sub>2</sub>, 15% glycerol to each 500 mL bottle, resuspend cells, and transfer into 3x50 mL falcon tubes.

12. **Final spin:** Spin cells down at 1500 g/15 min/4 °C (2800 rpm on JLA-10,500) (Whilst waiting, label up 40 (120 for 3 L) microfuge tubes or 8-track PCR tubes and put them in a cold room)
13. Decant supernatant off and add 4 mL (or 12 mL for 3 L) 85 mM CaCl<sub>2</sub>, 15% glycerol to each falcon tube, and resuspend cells.
14. Aliquot 105 µL of competent cells per tube using the repeater & 2.5 mL combi-tip (use slowest settings, and touch combi-tip to tube wall to help dispensing).
