# Native PAGE

1. To prepare the casting chamber, follow the protocol given in the SDS-PAGE, except do not add SDS in the separating or stacking gel Or have the option to use commercially available pre-casted gel to run the samples.
2. Prepare mini gel running chamber
    1. Add ~450 mL of 1x Tris-Glycine running buffer, up until the fill line. You only need to fill one chamber if running one gel (keep the chamber in a cold room or in a refrigerator to keep the buffer cold).
    2. Put the pre-casted gel into the running chamber (for commercial pre-casted gel- do not forget to remove the white label from the pre-casted chamber) with the wells facing forward.
    3. Remove the comb carefully.
    4. Wash the wells with the running buffer twice using a micropipette.
    5. Apply the ‘fun’ Invitrogen device to help you find the wells.
3. Prepare samples for NATIVE-PAGE
    6. Pipette 6 μL of 5x loading buffer (TG without SDS) into a fresh microfuge tube and add 24 μL of protein sample to it. Mix by aspiration. Keep it on ice.
    7. Load the samples per well.
4. Electrophorese at 100 V for 50–60 min in a cold room or in a refrigerator
