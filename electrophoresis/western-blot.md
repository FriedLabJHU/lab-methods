# Western blot

1. Prepare Tris-glycine buffer with 20% methanol. Methanol can be replaced with ethanol to reduce the toxicity of methanol.

| 10 x Tris-glycine buffer | 5 mL |
|---|---|
| methanol | 10 mL |
| MPW | Top to 50 mL |

2. Prepare TBST buffer:

| 10 x TBS | 50 ml |
|---|---|
| Tween 20 (must be fresh) | 0.5 ml |
| MPW | Top to 500 ml |

    1. After electrophoresis, Incubate the gel for 5 mins in Tris-glycine buffer with 20% methanol to make the gel compatible with the following Transfer step.
    2. Open the iBlot™ 2 Transfer Stacks, PVDF, mini<br>*There are two sizes of stacks in our lab, if you only transfer 1 gel. Use mini stacks, if transfer 2 gels, use regular-size stacks.*
    3. Take out the cathode layer with the cardboard (white blocking sheet right below the cathode).
    4. Roll the membrane gently with a wet roller to remove bubbles and wet it.
    5. Place the PAGE gel carefully on top of the membrane
    6. Roll the gel gently with a roller.
    7. Soak filter paper in MPW, then place on top of the gel.
    8. Place the cathode layer on top after removing the cardboard.
    9. Place the absorbent pad with an electrode on the very top. Ensure that the electrode tab matches with the metal piece of the iBlot device. Quickly close and latch the lid of iBlot.<br>*The transfer run may fail if the electrode tab doesn’t touch the metal piece closely. Open the lid and quickly close again to fix it.*
    10. Start the Transfer: <br>click template method P3: 20 V, 7 mins<br>*When running western transfer of high molecular-weight proteins, using the “hmw p” method will increase the transfer efficiency of large proteins: 20 V, 3 mins; 23 V, 4 mins; 25 V, 3 mins.*
    11. While waiting for the transfer, prepare 5% nonfat milk in TBST,<br>3% BSA in PBST for blocking works as well.

| Nonfat milk | 2.5 g |
|---|---|
| TBST | Top to 50 ml |

    12. After Transfer, check the membrane for the protein bands by looking through light reflection, protein bands are more shining on the membrane. <br>*sometimes there are green copper deposition on the membrane, one can wash with TBST to remove that.*
    13. Membrane for blocking: rock it for an hour at room temperature in 5% nonfat milk in TBST.
    14. Incubate the membrane with 10 mL of diluted primary antibody in 5% nonfat milk in TBST, overnight in the fridge with rocking.
        1. *1:5000 dilution of the primary antibody is enough to give a successful western blot result. Up to 1:1000 dilution can be used (this can be used at least three times when stored in -20 ºC).*
        2. *Alternatively, incubation at room temperature for 1 hour works as well*.
    15. Take out the membrane from the fridge and collect the diluted primary antibody in a 15 ml falcon tube and store it in the freezer for later use.
    16. Rinse the membrane in TBST 3x10 min.
    17. Incubate the membrane in 8 mL of diluted secondary antibody (1:10,000) in 5% nonfat milk in TBST for 1 h at room temperature.
    18. Collect the diluted secondary antibody in a 15 mL falcon tube and store it in the fridge for later use.
    19. Rinse the membrane in TBST 3x10 min.
    20. When ready for imaging, mix the SuperSignal West Femto Maximum Sensitivity Substrate chemicals in a 1:1 ratio to a total volume of 1.5 mL. <br>*We also have West Pico PLUS for imaging. This is cheaper. You can mix 5 mL+ 5 mL of the chemicals and incubate the membrane with the mixture for 5 mins. The signal will be lower than the above method.*
    21. Take the membrane out, allow the water to drain off the membrane, place on the black tray.
    22. Add the SuperSignal mixture to the part of the membrane where you expect to see your proteins
    23. Gently rock the membrane to fully coat it. Incubate the membrane for 1 min.
    24. Select the Chemiluminescence method, image with auto-optimization.<br>*one can also use Configure Signal Accumulation Mode (Enter: First Image 1 sec, Last Image 100 sec, images 20). However, this will consume a lot of memory space*.
    25. To image the ladder, choose Colorimetric mode, image with auto-optimization.
    26. In the gallery, select the Chemiluminescence and Colorimetric images, press merge. It will give a merge ladder and signals of protein bands.
