# SDS-PAGE

## Tris-Glycine SDS PAGE

1. Have the following buffers and stocks ready:
    1. 1x Tris-Glycine Running Buffer (takes ~450 mL per gel)
    2. 1.5 M Tris-HCl pH 8.3, 0.4 % SDS stock
    3. 0.5 M Tris-HCl pH 6.8, 0.4 % SDS stock
    4. 10% APS (keep for one month at 4 ºC)
    5. TEMED
    6. 40% acrylamide
2. Prepare the gel casting chambers
    1. Give glass panels a final quick clean using a little 70% EtOH and drying with KimWipe
    2. Assemble the sandwich with two glass panels and gasket.
    3. Place sandwich into casting chamber and lock into place
3. Prepare the separating gel. Use 8 mL per gel.

| % acrylamide | 10% | 10% | 12% | 12% | 15% | 15% | f.c. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| No. of gels | 2 | 6 | 2 | 6 | 2 | 6 |  |
| 1.5 M Tris-HCl pH8.3, 0.4 % SDS (mL) | 4.5 | 12.5 | 4.5 | 12.5 | 4.5 | 12.5 | 0.375 M (0.1%) |
| 40 % acrylamide (mL) | 4.5 | 12.5 | 5.4 | 15 | 6.75 | 18.75 | 10-15% |
| MPW (mL) | 8.8 | 24.5 | 7.9 | 22 | 6.55 | 18.2 |  |
| 10% APS (μL) | 180 | 500 | 180 | 500 | 180 | 500 | 0.1% |
| TEMED (μL) | 18 | 50 | 18 | 50 | 18 | 50 | 0.1% |
| Total Vol | 18 | 50 | 18 | 50 | 18 | 50 |  |

4. Pipet separating gel into the sandwich
    1. Tilt the casting chamber onto its heel
    2. Pipette 2 mL of separating gel, whilst leaning the casting chamber onto a corner, then slowly set the casting chamber back onto its heel. (Prevents bubbles from forming at the foot)
    3. Pipette the remaining 6 mL of separating gel
    4. Add 0.2 mL of isopropanol on top
    5. Wait 5–10 minutes
5. Remove isopropanol by tipping the casting chamber into the sink.
6. Prepare the stacking gel. Use **2 mL** per gel.

| % acrylamide | 3% | 3% | f.c. |
|---|---|---|---|
| No. gels | 2 | 6 |  |
| 0.5 M Tris-HCl pH6.8, 0.4%SDS (mL) | 1.25 | 3.75 | 0.375 M (0.1%) |
| 40 % acrylamide (mL) | 0.375 | 1.125 | 3% |
| MPW (mL) | 3.265 | 9.8 |  |
| 10% APS (μL) | 100 | 300 | 0.2% |
| TEMED (μL) | 10 | 30 | 0.2% |
| Total Vol | 5 | 15 |  |

7. Pipet stacking gel to the sandwich.
    8. Add the comb, and wait 10–15 minutes
    9. Prepare mini gel running chamber
        1. Add ~450 mL of 1x Tris-Glycine running buffer, up until the fill line. You only need to fill one chamber if running one gel.
        2. Remove gel from casting chamber and put into running chamber, with wells facing forward
        3. Remove comb
        4. Apply the ‘fun’ Invitrogen device to help you find the wells
    10. Prepare samples for SDS-PAGE
        1. Pipette 6 μL of 5x loading buffer into a fresh microfuge tube and add 24 μL of protein sample to it. Mix by aspiration.
        2. Incubate at 90 ̊ºC for 5 min to dissolve and denature
        3. Whilst boiling samples, remove comb
        4. Load 5–15 μL of sample per well
    11. Electrophorese at 200 V for 50–60 min (To get crispy bands, one can run the electrophoresis at 4 ºC, with 120 V).

## Tris-Acetate SDS-PAGE

Tris-acetate gel is optimized for large proteins. It is recommended to use the pre-casted NuPAGE™ 3 to 8%, Tris-Acetate gels to give reproducible results.

1. Prepare 500 mL of 20X Tris-Acetate SDS Running Buffer: 50 mM Tricine, 50 mM Tris Base, 0.1% SDS, pH 8.24.
    1. Dissolve the following reagents in 400 mL ultrapure water.

| Tricine | 89.5 g |
|---|---|
| Tris Base | 60.6 g |
| SDS | SDS 10.0 g |

    2. Mix well and adjust the volume to 500 mL with ultra-pure water. Store at 4 °C. <br>There might be precipitation after storing for a while. Warm the buffer at room temperature with shaking. The precipitate will dissolve.
    3. Before electrophoresis, dilute the buffer to 1X with water.
2. Prepare sample:

| Sample | X µl |
|---|---|
| NuPAGE™ LDS Sample Buffer (4X) | 2.5 µl |
| Deionized Water | to 7.5 µL |

3. Heat denaturing samples at 70 °C for 10 minutes.
4. Remove the comb and rinse the gel wells three times using 1X Running Buffer.
5. Remove the white tape near the bottom of the gel cassettes.
6. Place the gels in the mini gel tank.
7. Fill the chambers with the appropriate 1X running buffer.
8. Load the appropriate volume of your samples in the appropriate wells.
9. Load 5 µL HiMark™ Pre-stained Protein Standard.
10. Run electrophoresis for 1.5 h at 20 mA constant per gel at 4 ºC.

## Tris-Tricine SDS-PAGE

Tris- Tricine gel is optimized for small proteins. Instructions are provided below for electrophoresis of Novex® Tricine Gels.

1. To prepare 1000 ml of 10 X Tricine SDS Running Buffer: 100 mM Tris base 100 mM Tricine 0.1% SDS pH 8.3
    1. Dissolve the following reagents in 900 mL ultrapure water.

| Tricine | 179 g |
|---|---|
| Tris Base | 121 g |
| SDS | 10.0 g |

    2. Store at room temperature. The buffer is stable for 6 months when stored at room temperature.
    3. Before electrophoresis, dilute buffer to 1X with water. The pH of the 1X solution is 8.3. Do not use acid or base to adjust the pH.
2. Prepare 2x sample buffer: 450 mM Tris HCl 12% Glycerol 4% SDS 0.0025% Coomassie® Blue G 0.0025% Phenol Red pH 8.45

| 3 M Tris HCl, pH 8.45 | 3 mL |
|---|---|
| Glycerol | 2.4 mL |
| SDS | 0.8 g |
| 0.1% Coomassie® Blue G | 0.5 mL |
| 0.1% Phenol Red | 0.5 mL |

    1. Mix well and adjust the volume to 10 ml with ultrapure water.
    2. Store at +4°C. The buffer is stable for 6 months when stored at +4°C.
    3. The sample buffer doesn’t have a reducing agent. Add 500 mM DTT as 10X reducing buffer.
3. Mix the sample with the 2x sample buffer plus DTT.
4. Heat samples at 85°C for 2 minutes. Load the sample immediately on the gel.
5. Remove the comb and rinse the gel wells three times using 1X Running Buffer.
6. Remove the white tape near the bottom of the gel cassettes.
7. Place the gels in the mini gel tank.
8. Fill the chambers with the appropriate 1X running buffer.
9. Load the appropriate volume of your samples in the appropriate wells.
10. Load Spectra™ Multicolor Low Range Protein Ladder.
11. Run electrophoresis for 1.5 h at 20 mA constant per gel at 4 ºC.
