# Agarose DNA gel electrophoresis

1. In a 250 mL flask, add agarose (1% w/v) and 0.5X TBE. Note: 25 mL per gel. For example: For two gels, use 0.5 g agarose and 50 mL 0.5X TBE.
2. Microwave until boiling (~1 min).
3. Put the mixture into the 42 ºC water bath until it is cool enough to touch the bottom of the flask. [If leaving flask in bath, keep lid CLOSED.]
4. Add SybrSafe to the mixture (1:10,000 dilution) and swirl to mix. For example: For 50 mL, use 5 µL.
5. Set up the casting chamber with the gel loading tray and the comb.
6. Add ~23/24 mL of your gel mixture into the gel loading tray using a serological pipette.
7. Wait for the gel to set (~10 min; it will become opaque).
8. Take the comb out carefully.
9. Immerse the gel in 0.5X TBE running buffer in the electrophoresis chamber. The running buffer must cover the wells (~250 mL 0.5X TBE).
10. Add 5 uL of the DNA ladder (already prepared with loading dye) into the leftmost well.
11. On parafilm, mix each DNA sample with loading dye (e.g., 4 uL DNA and 1 uL 5X loading dye). Add this mixture to the wells.
12. Turn on electrophoresis chamber and run at max voltage (100 V) for 30 min.
