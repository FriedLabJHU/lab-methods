# Ni-NTA prep, storage and regeneration

### Regeneration of Ni-NTA column.

1. Wash the column with 2CV of Millipore water.
2. After water runs out, add 3 CV of 25 mM Tris 100 mM NaCl 50 mM EDTA pH 8. The column will turn white from blue.
3. Wash with 1 l of Millipore water. No EDTA should be there after wash.
4. Add 50 ml of 100 mM NiSO4. 6H2O.
5. Wash with 50 ml of Millipore water.
6. The column can be stored with 20% EtOH at 4 ̊C for use later or a fresh round of purification can be started.

   > Note: Sreemantee suggets that this be done after every single use of a column for purification.  Technically not necessary if you are going to use the column over again to re-prep the same protein.  MInimally, must be done
