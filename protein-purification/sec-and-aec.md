### Size exclusion chromatography.

| · | We have Superdex 200 (10 kDa – 600 kDa, user guide https://cdn.cytivalifesciences.com/api/public/content/digi-16443-pdf ) and Superdex 75 (3 kDa – 70 kDa, user guide https://cdn.cytivalifesciences.com/api/public/content/digi-17947-pdf) in the lab. |
|---|---|
| · | Buffers used should always be filtered. |
| · | The column pressure is very critical and should be always monitored and the limit should also be set in the Akta. |

1. Wash the column with 2 CV of Millipore water. Flow rate 0.3-0.4 ml/min (refer user guide).
2. Equilibrate with 2-3 CV of equilibration buffer. pH can be selected depending on the pH of the protein. The buffer must have at least 140 mM NaCl to minimize interaction with beads. Flow rate 0.3-0.4 ml/min (follow user guide).
3. Attach loop for loading sample into akta. For our columns the maximum volume is 500 ul (refer respective user guides).
4. Make sure the injection valve is in ‘Load’ mode. Clean the loop by injecting Millipore water 3-4 times.
5. After the equilibration is done, load the sample to the loop in ‘Load’ mode.
6. Change the injection valve position to ‘Inject’ mode so that the loaded sample can be injected to the column.
7. Collect the peaks automatically using the fraction collector or manually.
8. Check on SDS-PAGE.
9. If pure, concentrate and buffer exchange to appropriate buffers.
10. Make 10% glycerol stocks, make aliquots, flash freeze and store in -80 ̊C.
11. Wash the column with 2 CV Millipore water followed by 1 CV of 20% ethanol.
12. Store in appropriate box in 4 ̊C.

### Anion exchange chromatography.

| · | We have Q-XL fast flow columns 1 ml and 5 ml. |
|---|---|
| · | Sample should not have high concentrations of salt for AEC. |
| · | Buffers used should always be filtered. |

1. Wash column with 4 CV of Millipore water.
2. Have equilibration buffer (25 mM Tris 20 mM NaCl. Decide the pH depending on the pI of the protein) in Pump A and 500 mM NaCl in Pump B.
3. Equilibrate with 10 CV of equilibration buffer (25 mM Tris 20 mM NaCl. Decide the pH depending on the pI of the protein).
4. Make sure the injection valve is in ‘Load’ mode. Clean the loop by injecting Millipore water 3-4 times.
5. There is no volume limitation for the column. If using 1 ml loop, load sample in to loop in ‘load’ mode, change to ‘inject’ mode and let the 1 ml get injected to the column then only proceed with the next 1 ml, repeat this step unless you are done loading all the sample.
6. The desired protein should bind to the column but also collect the flow through to be run on SDS-PAGE later.
7. Wash the column with equilibration buffer until the OD 280 nm stabilizes.
8. Pump B has 500 mM NaCl. Give a gradient of 0-100% for 1 hour.
9. Collect all the peaks and run on SDS-PAGE.
10. If pure, concentrate and buffer exchange to appropriate buffers.
11. Make 10% glycerol stocks, make aliquots, flash freeze in liquid nitrogen and store in -80 ºC.
12. Wash the column with 2 CV Millipore water followed by 1 CV of 20% ethanol.
13. Store in appropriate box in 4 ºC.
