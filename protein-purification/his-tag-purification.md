# Protein expression and His-tag purification

*Contributed by Sreemantee*

### His-tagged protein purification.

1. Primary *E. coli* culture (antibiotic as needed, Amp final concentration 100 ug/ml) is grown till saturation at 37 ̊C. This would typically take 8-10 hours (growing O/N also works).
2. Add 5 ml culture to 2 l flask containing 500 ml LB media.
3. Grow at 37 ̊C till OD at 600 nm reaches 0.5-0.7.
4. Induce with IPTG to a final concentration of 0.5-1 mM. After induction the growth can be done at 37 ̊C or 30 ̊C. Time of induction can be varied depending on the protein being expressed.
5. Harvest the cells by centrifuging at 3500 rpm for 15 mins.
6. At this point the pellet can be stored at -80 ̊C for up to a week.
7. Here onwards all the processing should be done at 4 ̊C (on ice).
8. Resuspend the cells in lysis buffer. 25 mM Tris 500 mM NaCl, pH 8 works well for Ni column. Add DnaseI to a final concentration of 100 ug/ml and a tablet of EDTA-free protease inhibitor for every liter of culture. If the protein has -SH groups up to 1 mM β-mercaptoethanol can be used.
9. Sonicate for 15 mins (2min × 7, with 2 min gaps in between) 50% amp, 5s on, 5s off.
10. Centrifuge at 15000 rpm for 15-20 mins, in oakridge tubes.
11. Equilibrate the column with 10 CV of equilibration buffer (lysis buffer containing 2 mM imidazole)
12. Carefully decant the supernatant and incubate with pre-equilibrated beads for ~ 1 hour.
13. Collect the flow through.
14. Wash the column with 20-30 CV of wash buffer (lysis buffer containing 40 mM imidazole).
15. Elute with 1 CV (3 times) of elution buffer (200 mM imidazole in lysis buffer). Do a step gradient elution (50-200 mM imidazole) if required.
16. Check on an SDS-PAGE.
17. Do a buffer exchange to appropriate buffer for storage if the protein is pure or proceed to the next chromatography step.
18. Wash the column with 2 CV Millipore water followed by 1 CV of 20% ethanol.
19. Store in 4 ̊C.
