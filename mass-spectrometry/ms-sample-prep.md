# Mass spec sample prep

*Contributed by April*

Transfer your sample sample (x) to fresh 1.5 mL epi tube containing x mg of  urea. F.V ~x uL with f.c. of 8M urea (76 mg urea + 100 uL sample)

### DTT

DTT (f.c. 10 mM) 37 °C for 30 min at 700 rpm on a thermomixer to reduce cysteine residues.

### IAA

IAA (f.c. 40 mM) room temperature in the dark for 45 min to alkylate reduced cysteine residues.

### LiP- Go Directly Trypsin

### Just for Crosslinking

### Lys-C Digestion

XL: Add x uL of 0.1 mg/ml Lys-c stock in a 1:100 (roughly 0.4µg when doing the experiment at 40ug scale) and incubate at 37˚C/700 rpm for 1.5 hrs in the heat block shaker. vortex before incubation.

### Trypsin Digestion

Dilute the samples to 2 M Urea by adding x ml of  100 mM AmBic. To each sample, add trypsin at 1:50 (for Lip: 4uL of 0.1 mg/ml trypsin stock roughly 0.4ug when under 20ug scale) and incubate at 25˚C/700 rpm overnight (thermo block) vortex before incubation.

### Sep-Pak

1. Prepare x mL Buffer A and Buffer B.
2. Buffer A: 0.5% TFA in LC-grade water.

   Buffer B: 0.5% TFA, 80% ACN(LC-grade), 19.9% LC-grade water.

3. Add TFA to the sample to a final concentration (F.C.) of 1% TFA.
4. Condition cartridges with 2 mL of Buffer B. (10ml)
5. Equilibrate cartridges with 4 mL of Buffer A. (10 ml)
6. Prepare for loading using the vacuum.
7. Wash cartridges with 4 mL of Buffer A.
8. Remove cartridges from the vacuum manifold and place them on top of 14 mL Falcon tubes. Add 1 mL of Buffer B to elute.
9. In a 5910 R centrifuge, spin at 300 rpm for 3 minutes.
10. Transfer the eluted contents from the 15 mL Falcon tube back into an Eppendorf (epi) tube.
11. Speed vac down to dryness for approximately 3 hours.
12. Seal the epi tube with Parafilm and store the sample at -80°C.
