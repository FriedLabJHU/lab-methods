### Lip Steps

### Proteinase K

1. A 2 μL portion of a PK stock (prepared as a 0.1 mg/mL PK stock in native buffer) was added to a fresh 1.5 mL microfuge tube (enzyme:substrate of 1:100, with 0.2 µg of PK per tube when there are 20 µg of protein per tube).
2. Samples are then incubated for exactly 1 minute before being transferred to a mineral oil bath pre-equilibrated at 105 °C, and incubated for 5 minutes to quench PK activity.
3. Boiled samples are then transferred to a fresh 2 mL microfuge tube containing approximately x mg of urea to achieve a final concentration of ~8 M urea (76 mg of urea + 100 μL of sample will result in a final volume of approximately 152 μL at 8 M).
