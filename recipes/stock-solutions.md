# Stock solutions

## CaCl2 1 M

| Ingredient | mol | FW | amt |
|---|---|---|---|
| CaCl2 | 0.5 | 110.98 | 55.5 g |
| MPW |  |  | up to 500 mL |

1. Portion 400 mL MPW to a beaker with a stir bar
2. Ingredients mixed together
3. Top up to 500 mL final volume in graduated cylinder
4. Pour back to beaker to mix
5. Portion into 100 mL bottles
6. Autoclave 25 min.

## DEPC water

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| MPW | 1 L |
| DEPC (diethyl pyrocarbonate) | 1 mL |

1. Dispense 1L of MPW into 1 L bottles
2. Add 1 mL DEPC to each bottle
3. Cap and invert to mix
4. Incubate for ~2 h at room temperature in a fume hood, with occasional (every ~30 min) inverting
5. Autoclave 25 min.

|---|---|
| Glycerol | 100 mL |

## LB Agar

*for 250 mL, though scale appropriately*

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| MPW | 250 mL |
| Tryptone | 2.5 g |
| NaCl | 2.5 g |
| Yeast Extract | 1.25 g |
| Agar | 3.75 g |

1. Ingredients mixed together EXCEPT AGAR in beaker with stir bar
2. Adjust the pH to 7.0 (our materials tend to get very close to 7.0 already)
3. Pour directly into 500 mL bottle
4. Add agar straight into bottle
5. Mix up agar by inverting bottle a few times
6. Repeat as many times as required... >.<
7. Autoclave 25 min

## SOB

*for 200 mL, though scale appropriately*

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| MPW | 190 mL |
| Tryptone | 4 g |
| NaCl | 100 mg |
| Yeast Extract | 1g |
| KCI | 38 mg |

1. Ingredients mixed together
2. Adjust the pH to 7.0 with 20 μL 5 M NaOH
3. Adjust to final volume of 200 mL with MPW
4. Portion into 20 mL aliquots
5. Autoclave 25 min
6. To each 20 mL aliquot, add 0.2 mL of sterile 1 M MgCl2
